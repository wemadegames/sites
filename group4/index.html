    Then bake it

    1. Mix the dry things, then stir in the wet things until smooth.
    2. Pour the batter into a greased pan.
    3. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 30 minutes.
    4. Let it cool, then add frosting, sprinkles, and a cherry on top.
