How to Make Slime

A calm little activity for relaxation and fun. Follow the steps and make your own stretchy slime.

Steps

  1. Pour the glue into your bowl.

  2. Add the water and stir until smooth.

  3. Mix in a drop or two of food coloring, if you want.

  4. Stir in the baking soda.

  5. Add the saline solution a little at a time, stirring, until the slime pulls away from the bowl.

  6. Knead it with your hands until it is stretchy and not sticky.

  7. Stretch, squish, and relax. Store it in a sealed container.

Stay safe: ask an adult to help, wash your hands after, and do not eat the slime.
